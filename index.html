About Our School

Founded in 1985, St. Agnes Bukalango has grown to become one of the leading primary schools in the region.

Our History & Mission

St. Agnes Bukalango Primary School was established by the Sisters of St. Agnes to provide quality education to children in the Bukalango community. What began as a small school with just 50 students has grown into a thriving educational institution serving over 850 students today.

Our mission is to provide an excellent, well-rounded education that develops the whole child - intellectually, spiritually, emotionally, physically, and socially. We aim to nurture responsible citizens who will contribute positively to society.
